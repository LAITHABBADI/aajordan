Alcoholics Anonymous Meetings in Aqaba, Jordan

Alcoholics Anonymous (AA) has a presence in Aqaba, Jordan's only coastal city and a major hub for tourism, trade, and international residents. Whether you live in Aqaba year-round or are passing through, the AA fellowship offers free, confidential support through the 12-step program. Recovery is possible — and it starts with a single meeting.

About Alcoholics Anonymous in Aqaba

Aqaba's unique position as Jordan's port city and tourist destination means it attracts a diverse mix of residents, workers, and visitors. Alcohol is more accessible in Aqaba than in many other parts of Jordan, which makes AA's presence here especially important. The fellowship welcomes Jordanians, Arab nationals, and expatriates alike.

AA meetings in Aqaba may be held in English or Arabic, depending on the group. International visitors or expatriates are particularly welcome at English-language meetings. Contact the hotline to find the meeting format that best suits you.

Alcoholics Anonymous is not affiliated with any government, religion, or treatment facility. It is a completely independent fellowship run by and for people who want to stop drinking. There are no fees, no waiting lists, and no referrals needed. You can walk in to any open meeting at any time.

For those who are traveling or temporarily in Aqaba, online AA meetings are an excellent option for maintaining your recovery while away from home.

AA in Aqaba for Expatriates and Visitors

Aqaba is home to a significant expatriate community and hosts large numbers of tourists and business travelers each year. AA meetings are available to everyone, regardless of nationality or length of stay. If you are visiting Jordan and need AA support, here is what you should know:

  • AA is worldwide — the same 12-step program used in Aqaba is the same used in over 180 countries. If you attend AA at home, you will feel right at home in a meeting in Aqaba.
  • English meetings available — contact the hotline to find the English-language meeting schedule in Aqaba.
  • Complete confidentiality — your attendance at AA is never disclosed. Your employer, family, or travel company will never be informed.
  • Online option — if you cannot attend in person, online AA meetings are available 24/7 from anywhere in the world.
  • No registration — there is no sign-up process, no intake form, and no appointment needed. Simply show up.

The AA Program: Recovery Through the 12 Steps

The heart of Alcoholics Anonymous is the Twelve Steps — a set of principles for achieving sobriety and building a fulfilling life in recovery. The steps are not a religious creed, but a practical guide for personal transformation. Many members describe working the steps as the most meaningful and effective thing they have ever done.

Recovery in AA is not just about stopping drinking. It is about addressing the underlying attitudes and behaviors that drove the drinking in the first place. Through the steps, members take an honest look at themselves, make amends to those they have harmed, and develop a daily practice of honesty, humility, and service.
