About Alcoholics Anonymous in Zarqa

Zarqa is a major industrial and commercial hub with a large working population. The stresses of daily life in Zarqa — work pressures, family responsibilities, and economic challenges — can contribute to problematic drinking. Alcoholics Anonymous offers a proven solution that has helped millions of people worldwide, from all walks of life, achieve lasting sobriety.

AA is not a religious organization, nor is it affiliated with any political party, institution, or treatment center. It is a grassroots fellowship of ordinary people who have found a way to live without alcohol. The program is built on the Twelve Steps, which guide members through a process of personal change, honest self-examination, and service to others.

Meetings in Zarqa may be held in Arabic or English depending on the group. Contact the hotline to find a meeting that best suits your language preference and location within the city.

If attending in person is not possible, online AA meetings are available and follow the same 12-step format as in-person meetings.

Is AA Right for You?

Alcoholics Anonymous is for anyone who wants to stop drinking. You do not need to have lost everything to come to AA. Many people come to AA before they reach a crisis point — and that is a sign of wisdom, not weakness. Here are some questions to consider:

  • Has alcohol caused problems in your relationships, at work, or in your health?
  • Do you drink more than you intend to, or find it hard to stop once you start?
  • Do you think about alcohol frequently or plan your day around drinking?
  • Have you tried to cut back or quit and found it difficult to do so on your own?
  • Do you feel guilty or ashamed about your drinking but continue anyway?

If you answered yes to any of these, AA in Zarqa may be exactly what you are looking for. The only requirement for membership is a desire to stop drinking.

Why People Choose AA in Jordan

Completely Free

There are no fees, no subscriptions, and no payments of any kind. AA is self-supporting through voluntary contributions from its own members. Newcomers pay nothing.

Total Anonymity

Your participation in AA is completely private. AA's tradition of anonymity means your identity is never disclosed outside the group. What is said in the meeting stays in the meeting.

Peer Support

You will be surrounded by people who truly understand what you are going through — because they have been there themselves. The shared experience in AA is powerful and unlike any therapy or treatment program.

Proven Program

The 12-step program has helped millions achieve sobriety since 1935. Members who work the steps and stay connected to the fellowship consistently report long-term recovery and improved quality of life.
